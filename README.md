# Project 1 - Data-Driven Modeling

## Task 1: Propose a Topic (due: Friday, October 23 by 12:00pm CT)

Your goal is to:
1. Identify a data-driven modeling problem of interest to you that has some existing results you can compare to.
    - This task will require that you search for existing academic papers related to your problem.
    - You can set up a meeting with me to discuss potential problems if you are struggling with this.
1. Send a direct message to me on Canvas or set up a 5-minute meeting to discuss your proposed project idea. We will discuss relevance, scope, etc.

Topic requirements:
- The problem should have real-world value, even if that value is down the line (i.e., your work sets the stage for future benefits). The problem should not be just an academic exercise.
- You will need to compare your results to existing ones in the literature.
- The data you require should be attainable.

## Task 2: Implement and Document (due: Monday, November 2 by 12:00pm CT)

Your goal is to:
1. Implement your proposed approach to solving your problem.
   - I should be able to run your code to recreate your results.
   - Unless your data is really small, you should not include it in your submitted repository. Instead, please include a `README.md` file with clear instructions for accessing the required data.
1. Compare your results to existing ones in the literature.
1. Explain your results. For example, why do you think your approach was better/worse than existing ones? What were some potentially limiting assumptions? What improvements would you suggest if you were to continue this research in the future?
1. Document your work in a report.

Report requirements:
- Use AIAA conference format ([LaTeX template](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/preparation-of-papers-for-aiaa-technical-conferences/rsssbwthkptn), [Word template](https://www.aiaa.org/docs/default-source/default-document-library/preparation-of-papers-for-aiaa-technical-conferences-(1).pdf?sfvrsn=dd7be8a_0)).
- Sections: Introduction, Background, Technical Approach, Results, Conclusions.
- Maximum of 8 pages (not including references).

## Submission

1. Submit your code and report (as a `.pdf` file) to your GitHub repository.
    1. You can do this through GitHub in a web browser by: click on your repository > click "Add file" > click Upload files > drag your updated files > click "Commit changes".
1. Submit your assignment on Gradescope by submitting your GitHub repository.
